About Nickar Electronic Games '82

One developer, a lot of synthwave, and a soft spot for 16-bit consoles.

Nickar Electronic Games '82 is the studio of Nikolaos Karazafeiris, an independent developer making retro-spirited games and practical little apps.

Music producer, game developer, graphics designer, photographer and more. The games aim for the feel of late-80s and early-90s arcades and consoles. The apps and utilities start as tools I wanted for myself — and get released when they might be useful to you too.
