Super World Road Challenge '89

Real roads. Real curves. Pure arcade.

A love letter to the arcade racers of the late 80s and early 90s — but every road is real. 32 stages were rebuilt from real map data, with true turns, hairpins, climbs and drops, from the Amalfi coast to the Stelvio Pass, from Big Sur to the Karakoram Highway. Pick your cabrio, tune the radio and hit the road with Iva, your co-driver, calling every bend ahead.

Screenshots

Around the world in 32 stages

  • Europe, the Americas, Asia-Pacific, Africa & the Middle East — seven stages each
  • Four huge ring roads around Sithonia, Oahu, Jeju and the Cape Peninsula
  • Coastlines, deserts, forests, snowy passes, neon cities and tunnels
  • Day, sunset and night, with rain, fog, wind, storms and snow

Five cabrios

A '91 Italian supercar, a '70 American muscle car, an '89 German cabrio, a '92 Japanese roadster and a '58 Italian classic — each with its own top speed, acceleration, braking and grip.

Race your way

  • Time Attack — beat the clock from checkpoint to checkpoint
  • Race — up to 7 AI rivals, each with their own car and driving style
  • Elimination — the last car at every gate is out
  • Chase — catch the rival who started ahead
  • Championships — eleven cups, up to The Complete Challenge across all 32 stages
  • 1 VS 1 split screen — couch racing for two on one PC

Smash, boost, escape

  • Takedowns: ram traffic, rivals and police until they spin out
  • Nitro cans for the long straights
  • Police chases when you pass a speed trap too fast
  • Burnout launches — hold brake and gas, let go, leave smoke behind

Pure 16-bit style

Detailed pixel art, a CRT filter with scanlines, original synthwave and arcade music on the in-car radio, and a talkative co-driver with her own voice.
